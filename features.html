Everything in the game

What you will find inside.

Six things that make learning history here different from reading a textbook.

?

Quizzes

Short quizzes at the end of each part check what you remember. If you get one wrong, you see the right answer with a quick explanation.

◈

Puzzles

Match monuments to their names, put events in the right order and piece together maps from each era.

♛

Badges and rewards

Earn points for correct answers and collect badges as you finish chapters. Finishing a level unlocks the next one.

අ

Three languages

Play the whole game in Sinhala, Tamil or English, and switch at any time from the menu.

▲

Saves your progress

Your progress is saved on your phone, so you can stop and carry on from where you left off.

◎

Works offline

Once the game is installed, you do not need internet to play.
