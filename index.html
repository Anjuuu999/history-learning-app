The problem

Why history feels hard.

In school, history usually means reading long chapters, memorising names and dates, and writing it all down in the exam. A lot of it is forgotten soon after. We wanted to see if other students felt the same way, so we asked them.

Why we made it

Learning history
by playing it.

Most of us studied history the same way. Read the chapter, learn the dates, and hope the right questions come up in the exam.

Dust & Dynasties lets you go through Sri Lankan history yourself. You visit places like Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa, answer questions, solve puzzles and earn rewards along the way, in Sinhala, Tamil or English.

Inside the game

What you can do
in the game.

01 / YOUR CHAPTER

Matched to your class.

Choose Grade 7, 8 or 9 and the game takes you to the chapter that matches what you are learning in school.

02 / YOUR LEGACY

Keep track of your progress.

Your profile shows the chapters you have finished, the quests you have completed and the badges you have earned.

03 / YOUR LANGUAGE

Play in your own language.

You can switch between Sinhala, Tamil and English whenever you want.
