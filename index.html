The problem

History is full of stories.
School turns it into a list.

For a lot of students, history means long chapters, dates to memorise and an exam at the end. The stories get lost along the way, and so does the interest. We wanted to know how common that really is, so we asked students.

The story behind the game

History deserves
to be discovered.

A list of names and dates can only tell you so much. Behind them are real people, difficult choices, and places with stories of their own.

Dust & Dynasties is our way of bringing curiosity back to learning Sri Lankan history. We are building a game that invites learners to explore the past and connect with the stories behind it.
